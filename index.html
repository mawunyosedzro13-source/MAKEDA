ABOUT

"So who am I? Well, I'm a person going through an identity crisis right now, if I'm honest. But I suppose I'm a loving person. I smile a lot, but I have a naturally serious expression. I'm not really like my peers — not into everything that's 'all the rage.' I'm really good at adapting, because of the different sides of life I've been exposed to."

"I love God more than anything. I was taught to be independent from a young age. I love caring for people, and writing — writing is my main source of escape and therapy, even though people just don't get me. I'm misunderstood a lot, honestly. I guess you could say I'm beautiful, but I don't want to blow my own trumpet. I really love my lashes though — they're a nice highlight for me."
